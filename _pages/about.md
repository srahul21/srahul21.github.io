<p align="justify">
I am a postdoctoral fellow in the research group of <a href="https://cnislab.com/principle-investigator/">Prof. Jayant K. Singh</a>, Department of Chemical Engineering, IIT Kanpur. My work focuses on investigating the transport and interfacial properties of metal–organic frameworks (MOFs) for battery electrodes and ice recrystallization inhibition. Previously, I worked as a Research Associate at <a href="https://www.jncasr.ac.in/home">JNCASR</a> (Bengaluru, India) with <a href="https://www.jncasr.ac.in/faculty/bala">Prof. Balasubramanian Sundaram</a>, where I studied ion transport in MOFs. I completed B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Mathematics with a Ph.D. in Computational Physical Chemistry (Supervisor: <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/skreddy/">Dr. Sandeep Kumar Reddy</a>) from Centre for Computational and Data Sciences, <a href="https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/">IIT Kharagpur</a> (West Bengal, India). My research focuses on molecular modeling of physical phenomena using molecular simulations, quantum calculations, and machine learning.
</p>
While I am not working, I like reading books, cooking, spending time with family, and friends.
